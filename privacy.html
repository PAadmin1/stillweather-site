Privacy policy

Short version: Stillweather doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who we are

Stillweather is made by Samuel Voller, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: stillweather.apps@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your settings (chosen scene, view, sound mix, breathing rhythm, timers) are saved only on your own devices. If you use Apple Watch, your iPhone and Watch exchange these settings directly with each other using Apple's WatchConnectivity; nothing passes through our servers. We don't run any servers.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. They help us fix problems, aren't linked to you and contain no personal details.

Live Weather (Mac, optional)

If you turn on Live Weather, you choose a town. Its approximate position (the town centre, not your address) is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to fetch the current conditions, at most once every 30 minutes. Apple's privacy policy applies to that request. We never see it. You can turn Live Weather off at any time.

Purchases

Any optional purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and can't identify who bought it.

Photos (iPhone, optional)

If you choose "Save as live wallpaper", Stillweather asks to add one Live Photo to your library. It cannot read your photos.

Children

Stillweather is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future feature needs data, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details before it ships.
