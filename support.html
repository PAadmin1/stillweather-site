Common questions

How do I bring the controls back on iPhone?
Tap anywhere on the scene.

How do I use a scene as my wallpaper?
Adjust › Wallpaper › "Save this scene as a live wallpaper". Then go to Settings › Wallpaper › Add New Wallpaper › Photos, choose it, and keep Live Photo on.

The Watch app isn't showing.
Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Stillweather.

How do I add Stillweather to my watch face?
Touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit, swipe to the complications and choose Breathe, Sky, Stars, Moon or Hearth.

Can I keep it silent?
Yes. Sound is always optional; tap pause, or turn the volume down in Adjust.

On Mac, how do I hide the weather quickly?
Use Quiet in the menu bar, or its keyboard shortcut.
