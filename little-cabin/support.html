Little Cabin support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email stillweather.apps@gmail.com and we'll reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

Do I need to keep the game open? No. Your helpers and villagers keep working while you're away, up to a limit you can raise as the village grows.

Is there a timer or energy bar? No. Play for a minute or an hour; nothing nags you to come back.

I bought the whole valley on another device. Open the shop (the bag in the village controls) and tap Restore purchases.

Where is my village saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your village.
