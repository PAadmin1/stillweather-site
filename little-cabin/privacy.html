Little Cabin privacy policy

Short version: Little Cabin has no separate game account, advertising or tracking. Your village is saved on your device, and Apple services can provide iCloud saving, achievements and purchases.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

Stillweather: Little Cabin is made by Samuel Voller, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: stillweather.apps@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your village, progress and settings are saved on your device. When iCloud is available, village saves can also be synchronised through your Apple account so you can continue on another device. We do not operate a server for your villages.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may make crash and performance reports available to us. We use these to investigate faults. You can manage diagnostic sharing in your device settings.

Reminders (optional)

If you allow notifications, Little Cabin can remind you when a trial in the valley is nearly over. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no data leaves it.

Purchases

Unlocking the whole valley and buying an optional thank-you tip is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and can't identify who bought anything.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, achievements are reported through Apple’s Game Center service.

Children

Little Cabin does not ask children to create a game account or provide personal details. Apple manages its own account and purchase permissions.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future feature needs data, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details before it ships.
