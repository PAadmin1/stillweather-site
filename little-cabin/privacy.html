Little Cabin privacy policy

Short version: Little Cabin doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who we are

Stillweather: Little Cabin is made by Samuel Voller, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: vollersamuel@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your village, your progress and your settings are saved only on your own device. We don't run any servers and nothing is sent to us.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. They help us fix problems, aren't linked to you and contain no personal details.

Reminders (optional)

If you allow notifications, Little Cabin can remind you when a trial in the valley is nearly over. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no data leaves it.

Stillweather

If you also have Stillweather on the same device and support it, Little Cabin checks a simple on-device flag shared between the two apps to give you a thank-you lamp. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Unlocking the whole valley and any cosmetic packs is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and can't identify who bought anything.

Children

Little Cabin is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future feature needs data, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details before it ships.
